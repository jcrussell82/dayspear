Questions

Good to know

Straight answers about how DaySpear works, what it costs, and what it keeps.

Do my employees need to download an app?

No app store needed. DaySpear is a web app: employees open it from a link on any phone or computer. Anyone who likes can add it to their home screen so it opens like an app.

Will we get notifications on our phones?

Yes, with one caveat. On Android and desktop browsers, DaySpear can send notifications once you allow them. On iPhone and iPad, web notifications only work after you add DaySpear to your Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) and allow notifications there. You can also choose email or text for important updates.

What about team members without a smartphone?

Managers can print a clean, black-and-white copy of the published week for each location to hang on the wall. Team members can also get their shifts by text message whenever a new schedule is published, with no app or smartphone needed.

Can coworkers see my phone number?

Not by default. Your manager can see your contact details for scheduling. Coworkers message you inside DaySpear. If your business turns on number sharing, your number still stays hidden until you choose to share it.

Can I swap with a specific person?

Yes. You can offer a shift to anyone who’s eligible, or ask a specific teammate. Your manager approves it—or the team can turn on auto-approval for pickups.

Can I request part of a day off, or a whole week?

Both. Requests can cover a few hours, a single day, or a range of days. Your manager sees them on the schedule while they’re pending.

Does DaySpear replace Slack or our group chat?

Not necessarily. DaySpear keeps shift-related talk, announcements, and a simple team chat next to the schedule. If your team already lives in another chat tool, you can keep using it for everything else.

Is it a project management tool?

No. Tasks and checklists exist to support shifts—opening duties, closing duties, a few to-dos. There are no Gantt charts, sprints, or HR paperwork.

Do multiple locations cost extra?

No. Every example plan includes unlimited locations.

What personal information does DaySpear keep?

Only what scheduling and team communication need: name, photo, contact details, roles, locations, availability and notification preferences. Birthdays are optional, month and day only. No HR records, pay details or ID numbers.

Does it work in dark mode?

Yes. Everyone can choose light, dark, or match their device.

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

This policy explains what information DaySpear collects, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. DaySpear is run by Thinkigy LLC (“we”, “us”).

The short version: we collect what’s needed to run your team’s schedule and nothing more. We don’t sell your information, we don’t show ads, and we don’t use tracking or analytics tools.

1. Who’s responsible for what

When a business uses DaySpear, the business decides what information about its team goes in and who can see it. We store and process that information on the business’s behalf to provide the service. If you’re an employee and have questions about how your employer uses DaySpear, ask your manager or the business owner. You can also contact us, and we’ll help where we can.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, and mobile number if you add one. We use them to sign you in with one-time codes and to send you invites and account messages.
  • Work information: your business, locations and roles; shifts and schedules; time-off requests and the notes you add; swap, coverage and trade requests; availability and hours preferences; checklists and to-dos.
  • Things you choose to add: a profile photo, your birthday (month and day, only if your business turns this on and you opt in), announcements and messages, and notification and display preferences.
  • Billing information (owners only): your plan and subscription status, and the billing name, email and address you give Stripe. Card details are entered on Stripe’s page and stored by Stripe; we never see your full card number.
  • Contact form: the name, email, business name and message you send us.
  • Technical information: like any website, our hosting providers receive your IP address and basic browser information when you visit. This is used to deliver the site, keep it secure and prevent abuse (for example, limiting repeated contact-form messages).

Please don’t put sensitive information into DaySpear, such as Social Security numbers, bank or card details, or health information. The time-off reason field is for short, practical notes.

3. How we use it

  • To provide DaySpear: show schedules, deliver requests and messages, and enforce what each role can see and do.
  • To sign you in, keep accounts secure, and prevent misuse.
  • To send service emails such as sign-in codes, invites and important account or billing notices.
  • To process subscription payments through Stripe.
  • To answer questions and support requests.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We don’t sell or rent personal information, we don’t share it for advertising, and we don’t use it to train AI models.

4. Who can see your information

  • Your business: owners and managers can see team members’ profiles and contact details so they can schedule and reach people. Managers only see the locations they manage.
  • Coworkers: see your name, photo, role, locations and published shifts. They don’t see your email or phone number. The one exception is your phone number, if your business allows sharing and you choose to share it. Coworkers only see messages in conversations they’re part of.
